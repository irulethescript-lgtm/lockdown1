# Turn-by-turn attacking in Lockdown

1. Open a citizen's profile and click the crosshair **Attack** button at the top right. A warning asks for confirmation if they are more than five levels higher. This submits a POST, validates availability and immediately spends 25 Energy (15 with active Love Juice) plus 10% stamina. Opening a profile or battle page alone never spends Energy.
2. The five-minute battle timer starts on the server. Choose a weapon or unarmed action in the tray, then click **Attack**. Choose a temporary item and click **Use** to use it instead of attacking. One temporary item may be used per fight, subject to needle education requirements.
3. Each accepted click plays one round: your action followed by the defender's automatic response, unless they were defeated or prevented from acting. The log identifies hits, body zones, damage, misses, effects and reloads. Your Speed/Dexterity affect hit chance; Strength/Defense affect damage. Body zones use critical3.5x, major2x, arms/legs1x and hands/feet0.7x multipliers.
4. Guns require loaded ammunition. An empty gun cannot attack: click **Reload · 1 turn**, or switch weapons/Fists/Kick. Reloading transfers compatible reserve ammunition into that weapon and gives the defender a response. Attachments, exact rolled weapon stats, temporary debuffs, burning and timed needles carry through successive turns. The tray lists your owned usable weapons by category; unknown-stat weapons are unavailable. Kick requires Kick Training.
5. Defeating the target hides the tray and offers **Leave**, **Mug**, **Hospitalize**. Rewards are only awarded when you choose. Leave gives1.5x base XP and the most personal respect(3 plus a level bonus), Mug gives0.6x XP,1 respect and5–15% of carried cash, Hospitalize gives base XP and2 respect. Leave/Mug recovery lasts20–30minutes; Hospitalize lasts60minutes before modifiers.
6. Use **Back to Profile** or **Continue** after the result. Pending victories have a separate five-minute outcome window and default to Leave on expiry.

Fights end in a stalemate after25rounds or five real minutes, with no victory rewards. Timeout is enforced before accepting any turn and on state/page access. No background worker is needed. Already spent Energy, ammunition and items remain spent. Refreshing or restarting the app preserves the fight; sign in again after a restart because login sessions are held in memory. Versioned turn requests prevent repeated clicks from replaying a turn, and database transactions commit HP, ammunition, effects and logs together. Players cannot start overlapping fights or change equipment, heal, travel or move money during combat/pending outcomes. A separate10-minute cooldown applies per target.

## Boundaries

This implements the requested individual attack sequence. The opponent responds automatically. Escape, simultaneous group attacks, distraction, faction-war respect multipliers and NPC boss encounters remain outside this implementation. Respect awards use Lockdown's existing personal respect stat. Damage/accuracy balancing uses Lockdown's combat model, rather than claiming to reproduce Torn's private formula.
